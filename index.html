A bit about me

Marketer by data, designer by instinct.

I'm Sasi Kumar, based in Tamil Nadu. I started out designing posters for local shops, got curious about why some ads worked and others didn't, and ended up falling for the whole game — ads, analytics, SEO and the design that ties it together.

Two and a half years in, I've worked with jewellery showrooms, clinics, coaching centres and small D2C brands. What hasn't changed: I treat your budget like mine, I say no to things that won't work, and I'd rather under-promise and over-deliver.

When I'm not in Ads Manager, I'm probably redesigning a layout that nobody asked me to fix.
